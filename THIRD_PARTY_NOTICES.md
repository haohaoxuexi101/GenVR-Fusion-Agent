# Third-party notices and provenance checklist

The repository uses NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Numba, PyTorch and pytest under their respective licenses. Their versions are recorded in `environment.yml` and the official runtime is recorded in `submission/runs/official_llm/run_manifest.json`.

The two CFM checkpoint files referenced by `configs/ray_agent_core_llm.json` are team-generated artifacts. They were trained entirely with the in-repo pipelines: `scripts/01_generate_boundary_data.py` → `scripts/02_train_boundary_cfm.py` for the boundary model and `scripts/08_generate_internal_data.py` → `scripts/09_train_internal_cfm.py` for the internal model, with paper-scale resumable training via `scripts/32_train_cfm_resumable.py` (`scripts/15_paper_scale_reproduce.sh`, `scripts/16_slurm_paper_scale.sbatch`). The training data are programmatically generated by `gmc/benchmarks2d.py` and `gmc/mc_cell.py`; no external or third-party dataset is used. The checkpoints are therefore owned by the team and redistributed under the repository MIT License. Their SHA-256 hashes are frozen in `submission/runs/official_llm/run_manifest.json`.

The benchmark implementation and agent code added for this work are distributed under the repository MIT License. Scientific papers are cited as literature only; no paper text or figure is redistributed.
